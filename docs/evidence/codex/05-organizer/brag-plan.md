# Brag plan: COMPA — 05-organizer

Full `/brag --full` workflow, Hyperframes 0.8.216. No voiceover.

## Planning rubric

1. App: COMPA keeps conference connections and conversation context through personal QR codes, confirmed requests and a shared live board.
2. Strongest claim: “Meet people at the event. Remember them after.” (production landing capture).
3. Hook: Organiza el encuentro. COMPA guarda el vínculo.
4. Actual UI: admin.jpg and events.jpg; original mock artifacts or the public Codex Conf test event. No JSConf footage.
5. Minimum satisfying length: 20 seconds.
6. Tone: polished, playful conference energy with hard shadows and the product's rounded type.
7. Audio: warm instrumental bed, two restrained motion-matched accents. No narration.
8. Share caption: Abre la puerta a tu comunidad. COMPA: A QR door. An organizer console.
9. User flow: event door → personal QR → confirmed connection → saved people and event board. Scenes are edited demonstrations, not proof of a new real transaction.
