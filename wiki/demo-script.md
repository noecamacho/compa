# Demo and pitch for JSConf (p. 19)

The demo lasts 3 minutes and the audience takes part: they scan, connect with each other and watch `/live` move.

| Time | What happens | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | "How many people did you meet today? Do you remember what you talked about?" | Title and problem |
| 0:20–0:50 | Everyone scans the QR and creates a profile (name only) | `/live` with the counter going up |
| 0:50–1:30 | Ask people to connect with whoever is next to them | Podium and topics moving |
| 1:30–2:10 | A team member dictates a summary live and approves it | Card, summary and +25 on the podium |
| 2:10–2:40 | Organizer view: "this is what your community is interested in" | Topics and counters |
| 2:40–3:00 | How we used the coding agent (4 threads, worktrees, `AGENTS.md`) and ask for the vote | QR + project name |

## Key lines

- "No login, no LinkedIn, in two taps."
- "You just generated the data no organizer has: what people talk about in the hallway."

## Before going on stage

Seeds on, simulator on, `/live` in full screen, backup video open in another tab.
