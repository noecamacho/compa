# COMPA — overview

COMPA is a networking companion for conferences. An attendee fills in a profile once, connects with someone by scanning their QR code or typing their @tag, and at the end both receive a summary of what they talked about. Everything shows live on a Kahoot-style dashboard with a leaderboard and the most-discussed topics (p. 1).

**One-line pitch:** turn the conference hallway into data, without anyone having to pull up LinkedIn (p. 1).

**Design rule:** from opening the app to a completed connection in 2 taps, with no account, no install and no login (p. 1).

## Why the plan believes it can win (p. 1)

- The audience is the user. At JSConf people scan the QR on the screen, connect with each other and watch the podium and topics move with their own actions. Voting and using the app are the same thing.
- Real pain with a clear customer. Attendees forget who they met and do not follow up; organizers do not know what their community talks about.
- Meaningful use of coding agents: built with parallel threads in worktrees, an `AGENTS.md` as the contract and a skill, documented in the README.

## Goal of the build (p. 1)

A complete, demonstrable end-to-end flow: profile → connection → summary → points → live dashboard.
