# Success metrics for the demo (p. 2)

- A new attendee creates a profile and connects in under 60 seconds.
- The `/live` dashboard updates in under 2 seconds after each connection or summary.
- During the demo, at least 20 people in the audience connect with each other.

Per-screen performance targets live in [screens](screens/README.md) (for example `/me` loads in under 1 s on 4G, p. 5; both parties see each other's card in under 2 s, p. 5).
