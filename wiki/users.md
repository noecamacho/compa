# Users and problem

## Problem (p. 1)

- At a conference you meet many people and afterwards you do not remember who was who or what you talked about.
- Exchanging contact details is slow (searching LinkedIn, spelling usernames) and almost nobody follows up.
- The organizer measures attendance and check-ins but does not know what the community is really interested in or who connected with whom.

## Users (p. 2)

| User | Needs | Receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attendee | Connect fast and remember | The other person's card with their social links and a summary of the conversation |
| Organizer | Know what is happening at the event | Live dashboard with topics, connections and leaderboard |
| Audience in the room (demo) | Understand in 60 seconds | Podium and topics moving with their own actions |
